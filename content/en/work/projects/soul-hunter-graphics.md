Soul Hunter is a tabletop role-playing game adapted from a popular Chinese web novel. I was deeply involved in its crowdfunding campaign on Modian, a Chinese crowdfunding platform, providing product packaging, promotional graphics, and character sheet design. The campaign successfully raised approximately USD 12,000.

Find out more about Soul Hunter TRPG:
[Soul Hunter crowdfunding page on Modian](https://zhongchou.modian.com/item/148856.html)
